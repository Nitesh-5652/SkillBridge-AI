# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# SkillBridge AI

**Tagline:** Bridge Your Skills to Your Dream Career

---

# 1. Introduction

SkillBridge AI is an AI-powered Career Intelligence Platform designed to help students and professionals improve their resumes, identify missing skills, prepare for technical interviews, and receive AI-driven career guidance.

The platform combines Resume Intelligence, ATS Analysis, Skill Gap Detection, AI Mock Interviews, and Personalized Learning Roadmaps into a single application.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Job seekers often struggle because they do not know:

- Why their resume gets rejected
- Which skills are missing
- How Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) evaluate resumes
- How to prepare effectively for interviews
- Which technologies they should learn next

SkillBridge AI solves these challenges using Artificial Intelligence.

---

# 3. Vision

Build an intelligent career assistant that helps every developer become interview-ready.

---

# 4. Objectives

The platform should allow users to:

- Register and securely authenticate
- Upload resumes
- Improve ATS scores
- Identify missing skills
- Practice AI interviews
- Receive AI feedback
- Track interview preparation
- Follow personalized learning roadmaps
